Bite AI Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026 · Church Street Studios

Bite AI helps you track what you eat. This policy explains exactly what leaves your phone, who receives it, and how to get rid of it. It matches the privacy label on our App Store page — if the two ever disagree, tell us and we will fix it.

We never ask for your precise location, and the app requests no location permission. We do not collect your contacts, your browsing history, or any advertising identifier.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple sends us your name and an email address. If you pick Hide My Email, the address we receive is Apple's private relay address, and we never see your real one. That is the only identity information we hold.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health, Bite AI writes the calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat from meals you log into Health, and reads back your dietary energy for the day. This stays between your device and Apple Health. We do not upload your Health records to our servers or send them to anyone. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Bite AI.

How your food photos are used

When you scan a meal, the photo is sent to our AI providers — OpenAI and Anthropic — purely to estimate its nutrition, and the food names are looked up in nutrition databases (Nutritionix, and the USDA FoodData Central database). Your photos are not used to train AI models and are never shared for advertising. Photos you keep on a logged meal are stored on your device.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase — hosts your account and, if enabled, your synced logs.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic — analyze scan photos and food descriptions.
  • Nutritionix and USDA FoodData Central — nutrition lookups.
  • Mixpanel — anonymous product analytics.
  • Superwall — runs the subscription screen.
  • Apple — handles all billing. We never see your payment details.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. Nothing in Bite AI tracks you across other companies' apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Delete any logged meal in the app at any time.
  • Profile → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and every row of your data from our servers. This cannot be undone.
  • Profile → Reset All Data clears everything stored on the device.
  • Turn off Apple Health access in iOS Settings.
  • Email us and we will delete your data on request.

Children

Bite AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before the change ships.
